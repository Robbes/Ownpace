## What this is

The prerequisites are the part of a migration this product does not control: they happen in
Box's, Dropbox's, Google's or Microsoft's console, and often need an administrator who is not
the person doing the migration. So they are the part that gets interrupted, and until now the
product's guidance for them was a paragraph in a form that forgets.

The design decision worth keeping: **definitions in code, state in the ledger.** It means the
wording of a step can follow a provider renaming a console button without a data migration, and
that a checklist gains new steps for everyone the moment they ship. The one rule it imposes is
that a step's `key` is an identity — renaming one silently orphans every tick a customer has
made — which is why the keys are pinned by test.
